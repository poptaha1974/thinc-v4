## Editorial hierarchy

Resolve decisions in this order:

1. purpose and audience;
2. story or argument;
3. evidence and continuity;
4. performance and emotion;
5. shot order and duration;
6. sound story;
7. graphics and captions;
8. color and polish;
9. delivery and QC.

Later layers cannot compensate for failure in earlier layers.

## Footage audit

| Field | Inspect |
|---|---|
| Technical | duration, frame rate, aspect ratio, resolution, codec, audio channels |
| Story | objective, conflict/tension, progression, payoff, CTA |
| Performance | specificity, emotion, eye line, pauses, confidence, naturalness |
| Proof | use, mechanism, before/after continuity, product identity, results |
| Coverage | wide/medium/close, hands, details, reactions, environment, room tone |
| Risks | rights, claims, mismatches, flicker, clipping, focus, noise, continuity |

For large footage sets, inspect proxies or contact sheets first, then open promising clips at full quality.

## Paper edit

Write the viewer questions in order. A cut should answer the current question or create the next one.

Example for a product story:

1. Why should I stop?
2. Is this my problem or desire?
3. What changed?
4. How did it change?
5. Can I believe it?
6. Why this option?
7. What should I do now?

Use a two-pass assembly:

- **Radio cut:** dialogue, VO, silence, and sound logic work without pictures.
- **Picture cut:** visuals add evidence, emotion, orientation, or contrast rather than merely illustrating every noun.

## Cutting language

| Technique | Use for | Avoid when |
|---|---|---|
| Hard cut | clarity, energy, most transitions | continuity error becomes distracting |
| J-cut | pull viewer into next idea/place | incoming audio confuses current image |
| L-cut | preserve emotional or explanatory flow | outgoing audio delays necessary reset |
| Match cut | connect shape, motion, meaning | similarity is decorative only |
| Jump cut | compress performance or time | authenticity or comprehension suffers |
| Cutaway/B-roll | proof, context, conceal necessary trim | unrelated stock weakens credibility |
| Montage | compress process or accumulation | steps require full comprehension |
| Speed change | show duration, impact, or motion emphasis | it falsifies product performance |
| Hold/silence | emotion, comprehension, anticipation | it becomes unintentional dead air |

Let motivation determine transition style. The audience should feel the change before noticing the transition.

## Sound, captions, and color

### Dialogue chain

Use only what the recording needs: clip gain -> repair -> EQ -> compression -> de-ess -> limiting/true-peak protection. Compare with bypass to catch overprocessing.

### Music arc

Map music to the emotional job: intrigue, tension, momentum, relief, confidence, urgency, reflection. Cut to phrasing and energy, not every beat. Preserve silence when it improves credibility or meaning.

### Captions

- Break by semantic phrase.
- Keep one or two lines.
- Avoid orphaned particles and awkward Arabic line breaks.
- Match timing to speech and reading time.
- Use punctuation and spelling intentionally.
- Build contrast without covering evidence or faces.
- Deliver burned-in captions and/or a separate caption file according to platform needs.

### Color

Normalize camera transforms when required; balance exposure and white balance; match shots; protect skin and product; then apply a restrained look. Check waveform, vectorscope, and RGB parade when available.

## QC scorecard

Score 0-5 and explain any score under 4:

| Dimension | Question |
|---|---|
| Objective | Does every major beat serve one outcome? |
| Clarity | Can the viewer follow without rewatching? |
| Story | Does tension progress to payoff? |
| Proof | Are claims supported by what is shown? |
| Rhythm | Do changes follow meaning and emotion? |
| Sound | Is dialogue effortless to understand? |
| Graphics | Are hierarchy and dwell time clear? |
| Authenticity | Does the edit preserve believable performance? |
| Platform fit | Is the version native to placement and canvas? |
| Compliance | Are rights, claims, offer, and product identity accurate? |
| Technical | Does the exported file play and meet delivery needs? |
