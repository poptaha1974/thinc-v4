# Delivery, Platform Fit, and Learning Sources

## Delivery principles

- Follow the platform or client's current specification; verify when accuracy can change.
- Use the source frame rate unless conforming is deliberate.
- Match sequence color management to source and delivery.
- Keep masters and platform derivatives separate.
- Preserve an editable project, clean media links, fonts/licenses, and caption files when handoff is requested.
- Record export settings, version name, date, aspect ratio, duration, and approval state.

Typical social starting point—not a substitute for current specs:

- vertical canvas: 9:16;
- H.264 in MP4 when broadly compatible delivery is needed;
- AAC audio;
- burned-in captions when platform playback or audience behavior needs them;
- critical text, logos, products, and CTA inside platform safe zones;
- review on an actual phone.

For long-form delivery, supply captions separately when accessibility, search, or localization benefits from them. Measure integrated loudness and true peak against the destination's requirement; avoid claiming a universal target.
