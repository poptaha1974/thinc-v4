## Learning spine

Structure around learner change:

`why this matters -> prior model/problem -> new model -> worked example -> practice/application -> recap`

Define one learning outcome per section. Cut repetition while preserving context, definitions, causal links, and reflective pauses.

## Pacing and cognitive load

Do not import ad pacing into instruction. Attention is not constant motion; it is successful prediction, comprehension, and meaningful change.

- Change visuals at concept boundaries.
- Hold diagrams long enough to inspect.
- Use pauses after dense definitions or surprising conclusions.
- Remove filler words selectively; excessive removal produces machine-gun speech.
- Use chapter resets, summaries, and previews for long material.
- Signal why an example matters before presenting detail.

## Visual explanation

| Need | Visual |
|---|---|
| exact comparison | table |
| sequence or state change | flow/timeline |
| hierarchy or ownership | tree/diagram |
| quantitative pattern | chart |
| concrete procedure | screen recording or demonstration |
| emotional emphasis | performance, reaction, or relevant B-roll |

Reveal components progressively when the learner must follow order. Avoid stock footage that decorates without teaching. Use on-screen text for definitions, formulas, steps, and recap—not as a transcript duplicate.

## Interviews and podcasts

Build thematic arcs, not merely chronological cuts. Preserve the speaker's meaning and do not create a false statement by joining distant phrases.

- establish question/context quickly;
- use reaction shots ethically and in temporal context;
- smooth removals with room tone and motivated cutaways;
- keep breaths and pauses that carry emotion;
- identify claims that need source cards or editorial qualification;
- create chapter markers and pull quotes after the main cut is stable.

## Repurposing

Do not crop a long video and call it a Reel. Each short must work independently: one question/tension, enough context, one insight/example/payoff, and one relevant next action.

Rebuild graphics for vertical viewing. Reframe the subject manually, use larger captions, and remove references that only make sense in the full episode.
