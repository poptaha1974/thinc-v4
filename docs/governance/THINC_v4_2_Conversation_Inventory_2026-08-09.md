## Executive finding

The blind spot was structural, not a missing paragraph. Niche Family Tree existed outside the executable v4.2 path, while market evidence, media testing, persona, and economics were able to produce a generic top-level `PASS`. Reverse validation and post-test niche learning were also conflated under the word “feedback”.

The corrected release contract separates:

1. Discovery path.
2. Reverse validation before launch.
3. Convergence and strategic decision.
4. Independent market/economics/critical-risk gates.
5. Learning feedback after a controlled test.
6. Completeness governance.
