# Legal and Commercial Guardrails

This document protects THINC, Egy-Pioneers Academy, and AdMatch from overclaiming.

---

## 1. Ownership

All THINC references must preserve:

- Inventor: Dr. Ehab Taha.
- Arabic attribution: الدكتور إيهاب طه.
- THINC v4.0 naming.
- Watermark and ownership notices.

No output should weaken the identity claim.

---

## 2. Prohibited claims

Do not claim:

- Guaranteed sales.
- Guaranteed profit.
- Guaranteed first sale.
- Guaranteed job.
- Guaranteed insurance.
- Guaranteed campaign success.
- Guaranteed AI output accuracy.
- Medical, psychological, or neurological certainty.

---

## 3. Safer wording

| Risky wording | Safer wording |
|---|---|
| We guarantee your first sale | The program is designed to support you toward your first sale through guided execution |
| Guaranteed job opportunity | Top students may be considered for opportunities according to criteria and availability |
| Medical insurance included | Medical insurance access may be available according to plan terms and eligibility |
| This psychological trigger will make people buy | This behavioral principle may improve response and must be tested |
| AI will choose the winning product | THINC helps estimate product potential and update the decision with data |

---

## 4. Theory Registry disclaimers

Every theory should be treated as:

```text
A decision-support framework, not a deterministic guarantee.
```

Neuroscience and behavioral terms must not be presented as medical diagnosis or clinical certainty.

---

## 5. Academy claims

Academy OS can mention:

- workshops.
- community.
- AI tool support.
- mentor review.
- execution templates.
- possible opportunities.

But every conditional benefit must include conditions.

Examples:

```text
Available according to eligibility and operating policy.
```

```text
May be offered to top-performing students subject to availability.
```

---

## 6. Dashboard claims

AdMatch may show:

- calculated CPA.
- estimated profit.
- risk level.
- decision recommendation.

But should not imply:

- automatic campaign success.
- official Meta attribution truth.
- final accounting accuracy without confirmed source data.

Use:

```text
Estimated based on provided data.
```

---

## 7. Demo mode disclosure

If data is mock data, every relevant page must show:

```text
Demo Mode — numbers are illustrative and not connected to live accounts.
```

---

## 8. Human review

High-impact decisions should be marked as decision support:

```text
This recommendation supports managerial decision-making and should be reviewed before execution.
```

---

## 9. Versioning

Every report should include:

- THINC version.
- report timestamp.
- data source.
- demo/live mode.
- watermark.
